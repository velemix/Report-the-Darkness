More Information on Streetlight Issues

Understanding the causes, risks, and background of streetlight problems can help communities recognize why proper lighting matters and why these issues deserve attention.

Barangay Tejeros is a barangay located northwest of Makati City. This barangay is known for its love of Coca-Cola and has even been recognized as a top barangay in the recycling game.

But despite these, why do most of their residents consider it the most dangerous barangay in Makati? Well, this is because of the lack of properly functioning streetlights.

Broken streetlights were repaired in 2013, but the fact that the issue was only addressed once, years ago, and even highly advertised says a lot about the poor maintenance.

Alleviating streetlight issues can introduce many advantages. By artificially extending the hours of light so that activity can occur, it can be used to improve quality of life and increase security in urban areas.

Additionally, street lighting increases pedestrian, rider, and driver safety. Even though only 25% of all car trips take place between 7 p.m. and 8 a.m., 40% of fatalities and serious injuries to the same group occur during this time.

  • Increased fear
  • Increased risks of crime such as theft and kidnapping
  • Nighttime accidents
  • Unseen road damage that may cause injuries or vehicle damage
  • Route changes due to darkness
  • Reduced sight and visibility

Reduced visibility in the dark affects vulnerable road passerby and pedestrians. Because of this, it is necessary to identify the main causes for issues concerning the streetlights.

  • Lack of Streetlights: Lack of streetlights can affect the development of urban areas and the standard of living by limiting activities after dark. Additionally, the safety of motorists, cyclists, and pedestrians may be impacted.
  • Improper Maintenance: Streetlights that are not properly maintained can lead to increased crime rates, accidents, and a general sense of insecurity in the community. Regular maintenance is essential to ensure that streetlights function correctly.
  • Natural Disasters: Disasters can also damage streetlights, which is another reason why some lights may become nonfunctional. Typhoons, floods, and earthquakes can damage streetlights and disrupt their proper functioning.
